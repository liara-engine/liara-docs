Searching dev and latest of every module.The whole site shares one search index, rebuilt on every deploy over the development build and the newest release of every module. Searching from an older release therefore answers with the current documentation rather than with that release: its pages stay online and keep their URLs, but they are not indexed. Keeping one index instead of one per published version is what stops search from growing every time a release is cut.
